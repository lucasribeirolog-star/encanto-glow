Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Encanto Glow — Dra. Laureen Polidoro ("we", "our"), based in Sorocaba, São Paulo state, Brazil, collects, uses, stores and protects the personal data of visitors to this site or those who fill out our forms, in compliance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei nº 13.709/2018 — LGPD).

1. What data we collect

When filling out the registration form ("Client Area"), we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone / WhatsApp
  • Email
  • City
  • Date of birth
  • CPF (Brazilian tax ID)
  • Full address
  • Treatment of interest and any notes you choose to share

We may also collect basic navigation data (such as preferences saved locally in your browser) for the site to function, described in the Cookies section below.

2. How we use your data

  • To contact you to schedule consultations and procedures
  • Identification and issuance of documents related to your care (when applicable)
  • Keeping a treatment history for better ongoing care
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations applicable to aesthetic services

We do not use your data to send third-party advertising, and we never sell or rent it to any company.

3. Legal basis (LGPD)

We process your data based on your consent (when you voluntarily fill out and submit the form) and, when applicable, on the execution of preliminary steps for a possible appointment (art. 7, I and V, of the LGPD).

4. Who we share data with

Your data is stored in a private spreadsheet (Google Sheets/Google Workspace), accessible only to the Encanto Glow team, and is not shared with third parties for commercial purposes. We may use technical infrastructure providers (such as Google and hosting services) exclusively to operate the site and securely store data, acting as processors under the LGPD.

5. Cookies and local storage

This site uses:

  • Essential cookies/storage: used to remember your cookie consent preference and, in the internal area, to keep you signed in during your session.
  • Local storage (localStorage): a local copy of what you submit on the registration form may be saved only in your own browser, as a convenience record — this copy is not sent to us or to third parties.

We currently do not use advertising tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or social media pixels). Should this change in the future, this policy will be updated and new consent will be requested, when required by law.

6. Your rights as a data subject

Under art. 18 of the LGPD, you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether your data is being processed
  • Access, correct or update your data
  • Request anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent and request deletion of data processed based on it

To exercise any of these rights, contact us on WhatsApp +55 (15) 99280-9088 or on Instagram @dra.laureenpolidoro.

7. Security and data retention

We adopt reasonable technical measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, such as restricting spreadsheet access to authorized staff only. Data is kept for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose of registration and legal obligations, and may be deleted upon request, except where retention is required by law.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The date of the last update will always be shown at the top of this page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, reach out via WhatsApp +55 (15) 99280-9088.
